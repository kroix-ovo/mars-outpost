The two Outpost notification sounds are original synthesized tones created for
Task 03. No samples or external recordings were used. Both files are mono
22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM with 80 ms sine notes and short gain ramps:
confirm (520, 710 Hz), warning (470, 320 Hz). Distribute with the game under
the project's terms. Task 02 can incorporate this record into ATTRIBUTION.md.
