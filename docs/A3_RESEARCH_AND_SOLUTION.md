# A3 research and implementation solution

Research completed and solution laid out 2026-11-15, before runtime implementation. Scope: Task 01 A3 oxygen/water/CO2 processes, crew-demand accounting and reference parameters, on the integrated world/A2 branch. Construction, farming, EVA lifecycle, health outcomes and persistence remain separate work.

## Evidence and decisions

| Question | Primary evidence read | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| What crew baseline? | NASA's [2022 Life Support BVAD](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20210024855/downloads/BVAD_2.15.22-final.pdf), section 3.3.2.2, Table 3-31 and associated explanations | Use 0.895 kg O2, 1.085 kg CO2, 3.217 kg potable water per crewmember per Earth day for the reference profile. These are planning/model values for an 82 kg reference astronaut with an exercise schedule, not universal physiological measurements. |
| Why not 0.84 kg O2/day or arbitrary working multipliers? | BVAD discusses revised MetMan outputs; NASA's [CO2 technical brief](https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo-tb-004-carbon-dioxide-2/) reproduces a different HIDH activity schedule and totals | Do not mix models or double-count exercise. Apply the selected daily-average reference uniformly to idle/working base states until a detailed activity schedule is implemented. |
| What water can return? | BVAD Table 3-31 separates 1.420 kg urine water and 2.946 kg respiration/perspiration water from fecal water, food moisture and metabolic water | The reference profile collects 4.366 kg/crew/day into a lumped wastewater account, with explicit abstraction of perfect condensate collection and no hygiene/fecal-water recovery. This exceeds drinking draw because food moisture/metabolic water cross the modeled boundary; food inventory is kcal, not a complete body mass model. |
| Is 98% a single recycler efficiency? | [2024 ISS water status](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20240005472/downloads/ICES%202024-317%20Status%20of%20ISS%20Water_Final.pdf) and [2026 status](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20260004140/downloads/2026%20Status%20of%20ISS%20Water%20FINAL.pdf) describe UPA, WPA, BPA and humidity recovery as linked systems with differing recovery bases | No. Retain 90% as a documented game recycler abstraction, with 10% explicitly lost. Do not claim ISS hardware equivalence. |
| What does electrolysis produce? | [NASA-hosted 2019 electrochemical life-support paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20200002346/downloads/20200002346.pdf), introduction and electrolysis discussion | Model water to O2 + H2. Use the explicitly rounded 8/9 and 1/9 mass fractions already used by the fixture chemistry. This is stoichiometric bookkeeping with rounded atomic masses, not an equipment efficiency measurement. No Sabatier or hydrogen recovery loop is implied. |
| How is CO2 hazardous independently of oxygen stock? | [NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 Rev F](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/nasa-std-3001-vol-2.pdf), approved 2026-07-14, requirement V2 6004 | Its criterion is a one-hour average partial pressure no greater than 3 mmHg, with ventilation/local-pocket caveats. A3 implements an instantaneous engineering warning with a chosen 400 Pa threshold; it is not exposure averaging or medical certification. |
| Can kg CO2 be mapped to pressure? | [NASA Glenn equation of state](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/equation-of-state/), [NIST molar gas constant](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?r), [NIST CO2 molecular weight](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C124389&Units=SI) | Use the well-mixed ideal-gas approximation p = mRT/(MV), R = 8.314462618 J/(mol K), M = 0.0440095 kg/mol. The game's 100 m3 pressurized volume and 295.15 K temperature are explicit assumptions; map dimensions are not habitat volume. |
| What time denominator? | [NASA GISS Mars24 technical notes](https://www.giss.nasa.gov/tools/mars24/help/notes.html), updated 2023-05-15 | Mean solar sol = 24 h 39 min 35.244 s = 88775.244 s. Per-Earth-day physiology divides by 86400, exactly once in profile generation, never by sol length. |

PDF coverage was targeted, not exhaustive: BVAD first 110/235 PDF pages (including the needed metabolic/atmosphere sections); Rev F first 120/398 pages (including V2 6004); 2019 paper first 12/16 pages; water reports all 13 and 12 pages respectively. Relevant passages were read, not adopted from search snippets. The newer Rev F standard was checked because the online brief still references Rev D. The model-specific differences are retained rather than averaged into a fictitious universal astronaut.

## Concrete runtime solution

1. **Validated inputs.** Keep the eight synthetic initial fixtures and their hashes unchanged. Add a manifest-listed reference science profile with per-second values and provenance. Validate every used rate, activity table, output coefficient, threshold and capacity before initialization. Missing, negative, non-finite or unsupported data fails explicitly.
2. **Phase 3 unchanged.** Power dispatch still reserves electricity once, by priority and stable ID. Phase 4 never redispatches electricity when a later process is input-limited.
3. **Phase 4 life support.** Execute electrolysis, water recycling and CO2 removal by descending process priority then building ID. Common fraction = minimum of served/rated power, condition, available input/rated input and output headroom/rated output. Never multiply condition twice. Commit complete input/output maps through the ledger. Full O2 or H2 storage throttles the entire electrolyzer. Scrubbed CO2 leaves through an explicit removal sink. Recycling splits converted and lost water explicitly. Reclassify allocated load into useful allocation and idle/thermal loss without another battery debit.
4. **Phase 5 crew demand.** Only Task 01 deducts demand, once per living crew member. Allocate scarce base O2/water/food proportionally with stable-ID remainder handling. Suit draws remain individual, supported by synthetic tests; real EVA launch/travel and actual suit budgets are not implemented. Reference mode refuses unsupported EVA instead of presenting fabricated suit performance. Return detached requested/provided/unmet-need diagnostics for future crew integration.
5. **Off-nominal abstraction.** Scale modeled CO2 output by delivered oxygen fraction, and collected wastewater by the lesser of delivered water/food fractions. This prevents unlimited modeled output from empty inputs without pretending to simulate body reserves or medical response. Nominal synthetic reference scenarios remain directly comparable. Metabolism never stops because wastewater storage is full: retain what fits and record unrecoverable overflow plus a storage-full alert. CO2 is never storage-clamped.
6. **Phase 8 finalization.** Refresh actual resource deltas, available-stock depletion forecasts and persistent low-O2, CO2 and overflow alerts. Shortage entry emits a committed event; repeated unchanged shortages do not flood event IDs. Preserve pause, command boundaries, rollback, detached snapshots and bounded tick receipts. No new public snapshot keys.
7. **Authored map sizing.** Size each life-support process for six reference crew plus 20% nominal throughput margin; keep 90% recovery and existing power ratings as game choices. This corrects the old arbitrary per-second rates while avoiding an unsupported claim of measured equipment performance. Six-crew O2 design output is 6 * 0.895 * 1.2 = 6.444 kg/Earth day; water input is 7.2495 kg/day and H2 output 0.8055 kg/day. Recycler input capacity is 31.4352 kg/day; scrubber capacity is 7.812 kg/day. Actual operation remains input/output limited. Food draw is a rounded 3000 kcal/crew/day game target, contextualized by the BVAD's 12.778 MJ/day reference rather than presented as exact equivalence.

## Acceptance checks

- Existing initial snapshots, FIFO/control/callback/clock/pause/backlog tests and history-scaling protections remain valid. A2's electrical isolation tests explicitly isolate phase 3; full A3 integration is tested independently.
- Compare nominal synthetic 5/6/9-crew balances through 600 ticks, storage overflow/full-output snapshots, and the power-loss chemistry sequence against committed oracle fields and independent arithmetic. Do not rewrite generated expected values to fit code.
- Test mass ratios, explicit water/CO2/idle-energy losses, zero/partial power, condition, shared-input ordering, reservations, full oxygen/hydrogen tanks and no negative stock.
- Test mixed living/dead/base/EVA crew, proportional shortages, individual suit depletion, shortage events and wastewater overflow. Check rollback after phase 5, RNG/counters/receipts/signals/backlog, pause and equivalent frame schedules.
- Check normalized per-Earth-day values and the separate sol denominator. Verify the reference CO2 threshold from the assumed volume/temperature; label it as instantaneous.
- Run clean-copy Godot checks, fixture freshness, independent formulas, CI and exports. Report measured results and remaining limitations.

## Deliberate limits

This is a single well-mixed habitat/resource network. There is no pressure regulation, nitrogen/atmospheric mixing model, airlock loss calculation, medical exposure/dose response, detailed human body mass balance, contaminant/potability chemistry, pathogen model, dust event, real suit hardware, or measured equipment power/throughput curve. Those cannot be established merely by assigning a NASA citation to a placeholder. Research has resolved the A3 design decisions above; further retrieval would not change this bounded implementation. Real hardware selection, activity schedules and health/hazard behavior need later scoped work.

## Implementation verification

Implemented on `codex/a3-life-support`, based on the integrated world/A2 branch. Both local and fresh-project-copy runs without imported cache passed: WORLD 679, A1 14295, A2 electrical isolation 41144, A3 6718, world integration 54, bridge regressions 17 and full-runtime A1 large-journal benchmark 36565 checks. Godot import and headless main-scene boot passed. All 85 original reference JSON files remain unchanged and fresh; independent closed-form checks and both profile/manifest generators pass `--check`.

In the actual rendered Windows game, F3 loaded the reference scenario, resource rates and forecasts updated, selecting the oxygen generator at [16,26] and pressing T changed its electrical request from 1.5 kW to zero, and the next completed tick changed net oxygen from production surplus to crew-only depletion. Space paused the colony and the window was closed afterward. This check used the dummy audio driver; sound remains unverified. Clean-copy full A3 ticks measured about 1.33 ms with empty command history and 1.39 ms with 20,000 retained commands on this machine; this is not a universal performance claim.
