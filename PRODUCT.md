# Mars Outpost Survival

NASA Space Apps prototype for ages 7–12: build a small outpost, assign specialists and understand the resource consequences of farming, life support, power shortages and repairs. Godot 4.7.2, native Windows submission build; no runtime AI service.

The user requests a familiar Sims/Cities: Skylines management interface: minimal persistent information, stable labeled navigation, one contextual panel at a time, clear next actions and a visible world. The approved direction is dark slate/navy with restrained mint accents. White panels were rejected. Playback uses real drawn one/two/three play triangles with extra spacing. Astronaut portraits are close-up faces, with names and specialties in separate labels. No active-alert text may be clipped.

This submission is a playable Level 1 slice. Furnished pressure-zone wings, daily eating/sleeping, full medical care, research/upgrades, supply deliveries and a two-year campaign remain planned. The commander model is presentation; existing simulation crew perform the jobs.
